Please place your CAFFT exposure videos in this directory.
Naming convention: [video_id]_[language_code].mp4
Example: ev001_ca.mp4, ev001_es.mp4, ev001_en.mp4

The help videos are not in the repository. For development, link the team's
delivery here (both paths are git-ignored):
  ln -s <delivery>/videos_cafft/ajuda        public/videos_cafft/ajuda
  ln -s <delivery>/videos_cafft/cataleg.json public/videos_cafft/cataleg.json
